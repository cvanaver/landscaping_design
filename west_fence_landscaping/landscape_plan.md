# West Fence Landscape Plan — Condensed

One-page summary of the full plan. See `landscape_design_plan.md` for the complete spec.

## Site

- **Address:** 1013 Colfax St, Evanston, IL — USDA Zone 6a (lake-moderated)
- **Bed orientation:** East-facing; plants east of the west boundary fence
- **Sun:** ~5–7 hrs direct, all morning + early afternoon (fence shades the bed mid-afternoon onward)
- **Soil:** USDA Urban land–Psamments. Loam cap 0–10", pure sand 10–60". Excessively drained. pH 6.7–7.0.
- **Hydrology:** Briefly floods in spring (snowmelt + heavy rain), recedes within hours to a day. Sandy plateau, not a wet site.
- **Pear tree** at the north jut inside corner, ~7 ft east of fence, ~10 ft canopy spread — casts shade over inside corner of bed.

## Design

**Straight front edge across the full 46 ft, deep alcoves on the side sections, shallow at the jut.**

- North & south alcoves: ~63" deep (two rows of plants)
- Middle jut: ~30" deep (front row only)
- Front edge: one continuous straight line

## Soil & Planting (sandy ridge edition)

- **No tilling. No rock bar.** Easy dig on sand below 10".
- **Per-hole backfill:** 50% native soil + 40% compost + 10% leaf mold/peat
- **Grass holes:** 16" wide × 14" deep
- **Front-line holes:** 10" × 10"
- **Crowns ½" above grade** (spring flooding adjustment)
- **Broadfork the pear zone bed-wide** before planting geraniums
- **Mulch:** 3" shredded hardwood, 2" clearance around crowns

## Year-1 Watering

| Period | Frequency | Volume per plant per event |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | 4 events | 1 gal flower / 2 gal grass |
| Weeks 2–4 | 3× weekly | Same |
| Weeks 5–8 | 2× weekly | Same |
| Weeks 9–12 | 1× weekly deep | ~60 gal across full bed |
| Weeks 13+ | As needed | Same |

**Pear zone (geraniums): +1 extra watering per week all year 1.**

Soaker hose recommended. Check moisture with a screwdriver, not your finger.

## Don'ts

- Don't till
- Don't cut grasses in fall (March cutback only)
- Don't plant deep — crown ½" above grade
- Don't use cheap big-box "compost"
- Don't over-fertilize on sand (counterintuitive but true)
- Don't use plastic edging
- Don't crowd the pear with coneflowers
