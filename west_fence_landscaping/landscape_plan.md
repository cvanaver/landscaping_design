# Backyard Fence Landscaping Plan + Rendering Workflow

## Overview

This document includes:

1. A structured landscaping plan for a backyard fence line
2. Exact layout, spacing, and plant counts
3. A workflow to generate architect-level drawings and realistic renderings

---

# PART 1 — LANDSCAPING PLAN

## Fence Layout

- Left section: 168" (14 ft)
- Middle inset: 153" (12.75 ft)
- Right section: 228" (19 ft)
- Total length: ~45–46 ft
- Planting depth: 30–33"

---

## Design Concept

Two-layer system:

- Back row (structure): upright ornamental grasses
- Front row (color): single repeating perennial

Clean, linear, modern design with low maintenance.

---

## Plant Selection

### Structure (Back Row)
- Karl Foerster Feather Reed Grass (Calamagrostis x acutiflora 'Karl Foerster')

### Color (Front Row) — Choose ONE
- Coneflower (Echinacea purpurea)
OR
- Black-eyed Susan (Rudbeckia hirta)

---

## Spacing

### Grasses
- 30–36 inches apart
- Positioned ~6 inches off fence

### Flowers
- 12–16 inches apart
- Positioned in front third of bed
- Keep 4–6 inches off lawn edge

---

## Section Breakdown

### Left (168")
- 5 grasses
- 10–12 flowers

### Middle (153")
- 5 grasses
- 10–11 flowers

### Right (228")
- 7 grasses
- 14–16 flowers

---

## Total Plants

- Grasses: ~17
- Flowers: ~35–40

---

## Soil Conditions

- Top layer: workable soil
- Below: buried rock layer

### Instructions:
- Do NOT till
- Dig individual holes only
- Remove rocks inside each hole
- Backfill with compost/soil

---

## Installation Sequence

1. Remove rocks
2. Clean weeds/roots
3. Define edge with string line
4. Mark plant spacing
5. Dig holes
6. Plant grasses
7. Plant flowers
8. Mulch
